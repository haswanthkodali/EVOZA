Technology

Powered by AI & Smart Tech

Technology is the backbone of EVOZA. It enables seamless theme transformations, personalised experiences, and efficient operations — all from a central intelligence platform.

🧠
Artificial Intelligence
AI learns your preferences, dietary requirements, and past visits to personalise every future experience for you.
🎨
Theme Engine
A central scheduling system that plans, manages, and executes every theme transformation automatically.
💡
Smart Lighting
Intelligent LED systems that shift colour, intensity, and direction to match each theme in real time.
📽️
Projection Mapping
Walls, ceilings, and floors become living canvases — projecting immersive visuals that transform the space completely.
🤖
Service Robotics
Smart robots assist with service, delivery, and guest interaction — adding a futuristic layer to hospitality.
📱
Customer App
Book tables, track upcoming themes, manage memberships, and set dining preferences — all from your phone.
EVOZA Experience OS
Our long-term vision is to build a complete hospitality operating system — a central platform that controls themes, lighting, music, digital menus, reservations, and analytics simultaneously. Once proven, this technology will be licensed to other hospitality businesses worldwide.
